`packages/session/session-title-llm/src/index.ts:raceAbort` settles a title request at its deadline or caller cancellation even when the adapter's `prepareCall` or a pending stream `next()` ignores the abort signal and never settles (finding 324). Before this, the executor only checked the signal after each await, so a permanently stalled adapter left the request, its provider call tracked by the title service, and provider or service disposal pending forever despite the configured `timeoutMs`.
`packages/session/session-title-llm/src/index.ts:executeSessionTitleLlm` races preparation and every stream demand against the call deadline signal and rejects with the signal's reason (the `SESSION_TITLE_TIMEOUT_CODE` timeout reason, or the caller's cancellation reason). The abandoned adapter work is not stopped: its eventual result or rejection is discarded and the underlying promise stays the adapter's to release through the signal it was given. A late result can never reach the provider because the executor has already rejected.
`packages/session/session-title-llm/src/index.ts:executeSessionTitleLlm` closes the stream iterator on exit without awaiting it, because an async generator queues `return()` behind a stuck `next()`; awaiting it would reintroduce the unbounded wait. Any rejection of that detached close is swallowed.

`packages/session/session-title-llm/src/index.ts:executeSessionTitleLlm` (iterator cleanup): the discarded failure is the rejection or synchronous throw from `iterator.return`, which only closes an iterator the loop has already left. The catch names it `_returnFailed`; the outer `finally` keeps the original error.

- `packages/session/session-title-llm/src/index.ts:executeSessionTitleLlm` - the close error of the stream iterator is discarded: the loop has already left the iterator, so the error has no owner.
